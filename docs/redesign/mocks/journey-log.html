Log a meal, and every other quick log

Spatial Depth with the Minimise to Circle tab bar. Every phone is built at a real iPhone’s 390 × 844 pt to the 26 Oct sizing spec (title 30, body 16, rows 58, tab capsule and orb 52), then scaled to fit. Every phone is live: use the state buttons under it, tap through it, drag a sheet down, swipe from the left edge of a pushed page to go back. Four open decisions get four coded options each with a recommendation; the other screens get one recommended design. Numbers under each phone: taps and estimated seconds for the main job against today’s code, live tap-target and orange counts, and frame gaps measured in Chrome.

        All measurements

        Time model: 0.9 s per tap including pointing, 1.2 s per decision, 0.28 s per typed character, plus network waits (search 0.6 s, photo reading about 5 s, Geo 3-4 s). Tap targets and orange counts are audited in every state of each phone by Playwright; frame gaps are the longest gap between animation frames while the push, sheet and scroll run, median of 3 runs with the worst in brackets (16.7 ms is a perfect 60 fps frame). Headless Chrome on an M-series Mac with all 20 phones on one page: a pessimistic desktop proxy, not a low-end Android number (F6). The screen behind a push or sheet scales and dims but does not blur: an animated 1.5 px blur raised the median push gap from 18 to 27 ms and the worst sheet gap to 533 ms, so it is dropped (a departure from the o19 mock, cheap to reverse).

        5-user usability script

        1. Recruit 5 people who log food at least 3 days a week (2 iPhone, 2 Android low-end, 1 who has never used FIQR). 30 minutes each, recorded with consent.
        2. Open the prototype at the recommended options. Say: “Think aloud. We are testing the app, not you.”
        3. Give the tasks one at a time, in order. Do not help; after 2 minutes stuck, mark fail and move on.
        4. After each task ask: “How sure are you it worked, 1 to 5?”
        5. At the end: “What was the most annoying moment?” and the SUS questionnaire.
