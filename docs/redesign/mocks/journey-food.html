Review my food

The Food tab: how you read today, move between days, and fix what you logged. Built from the live diary code and the logs API, with real data shapes. The phones are built at real iPhone size (390 x 844 pt, 30 pt title, 16 pt body, 52 pt tab bar) and shown at 77%. They are live: tap, swipe, scroll, and use the state chips under each one.

Journey summary

Jobs, in order of frequency

  • See what is left today and what is already logged (several times a day).
  • Fix an entry: amount, meal, or a duplicate (most days).
  • Look back at a past day, or reuse it (a few times a week).
  • Check where the targets come from (weekly or less).

What changes against today

  • The summary leads with "left" today and "eaten vs target" on other days; it never shows a "left" figure for a past or future day.
  • Rows open a small action sheet: Move and Copy for one item become possible with no backend change.
  • Loading and errors stay inside the card, so the header and strip keep working.
  • Without a plan, the plan link names where the targets come from.

Flow map

Recommended flow

Recommended picks combined: A3 Remaining first, B1 Day strip, C1 Grouped cards, D1 Tap for actions. Try it: tap a row, Move, Lunch, then Undo; swipe the strip; open the calendar; tap "Your nutrition plan" and drag from the left edge to go back; scroll to minimise the tab bar.

Choices taken alone (say if any should change)

  • The orb is ink, not orange: Q4 says the orb is never orange, which overrides the o01 mock.
  • Cards are solid white, and glass is only on controls (tab bar, round buttons, sheets, toast), per Q3. The o19 mock has glass cards.
  • Delete uses ink, not red: Q2 allows no new hues.
  • Delete still asks for confirmation, then also offers Undo. Swipe-delete (D2) skips the confirm and relies on Undo.
  • "Move to another day" is left out: local_date cannot be patched, so it would need a create then a delete. Copy covers the common case.
  • Springs are critically damped, with no overshoot. The taste skill bans bounce on interface elements, so o01's bounce curve is dropped.

Taste-skill conflicts (brand wins, noted)

  • Cream ground with an orange accent is on the skill's banned list ("cream with terracotta"). Both are fixed brand colours (Q7), so they stay. Orange only marks data, plus Save in the edit sheet.
  • Glass and blur are banned on app screens in the skill. They are kept on the controls layer only, because Q3 and C1 are signed.

Screen cards

/food diary

Who: every logged-in user. How often: several times a day, mostly after logging and before the next meal. Source: src/app/(app)/food/page.tsx, components/diary/*.

Jobs: 1. See what is left today. 2. Check what was logged and fix it. 3. Look back at, or reuse, a past day.

On screen today: large title "Food" with the date fading between days; a round calendar button; a week strip (tick ruler, orange today marker, dots on logged days, swipe to change week); an offline band; a day hero (kcal count-up, thin orange line, "x left of y" or "x over y", three macro dot meters out of 10); "Your nutrition plan" (only when a plan is active); four meal groups (the header opens Add, Copy to today on past days, Save as meal); rows (name, amount, P/C/F, kcal); itemised photo logs expand into components with a pencil to edit each; dashed "Add breakfast" slots; "Add food" opens /logs/new/food. When Geo changes something in chat, the diary refetches.

States in the code: skeleton only after 150 ms (held so a fast load never flashes); full-screen ErrorState "I couldn't load this day"; "No daily target set"; over target; empty meals; first-paint choreography once per session.

Data: getLogs(type food, days at least 30, capped at 500 rows) + active meal plan + profile. Targets come from the plan, then the profile, then 0. food_name has no length limit; meal_type is free text (unknown values go under Snack); local_date can be up to 7 days ahead.

Today's taps: read today 0; yesterday 1; a day in an earlier week 2; copy a past meal to today 3.

Month view sheet

Opened from the calendar button. About weekly. Source: month-view-sheet.tsx.

Jobs: jump to an older day; see which days were logged and their totals.

Today: month title, previous and next buttons, close; a 7-column grid with day number, kcal total, today's orange triangle, a workout dot and the selected outline. It refetches food and workout logs for the visible month. There is no loading or error UI (cells just stay blank), and future months are not limited.

Today's taps: 2 (calendar, day), plus 1 per month back.

Row actions sheet and confirm

New. Today a row tap opens the edit sheet, and the only actions are per meal.

Jobs: fix a wrong meal, remove a duplicate, re-log one item on another day.

Today: delete sits inside edit (row, Delete entry, confirm "Delete this entry?"). Move between meals and copy one item are not in the diary at all, only in Geo chat. Backend: PATCH /v1/logs/:id merges payload, so changing meal_type works today; local_date cannot be patched, so "move to another day" means create on the new date, then delete.

Today's taps: delete 3; move: not possible (Geo chat, about 3 taps plus typing).

Edit a logged item

FoodSearchSheet in edit mode. A few times a week.

Jobs: correct the amount; correct one component of a photo estimate.

Today: title "Edit "; Servings or Grams chips; a stepper (±0.25 servings, ±10 g); four macro tiles; an orange Save; "Delete entry" with a confirm. You cannot change the meal or the date here. Photo components are edited inline in the diary instead (4 number fields, ink Save).

Today's taps: 1 step change is row, +, Save = 3; one photo component is expand, pencil, edit, Save = 3 plus typing.

"Your nutrition plan" link

Weekly or less.

Jobs: check where the targets come from, and change them.

Today: a grey text link under the hero, shown only when there is an active meal plan, which pushes /plans/view?id=. Users without a plan get no hint of where their numbers come from.

Today's taps: 1.

Usability script, 5 users

Five people who log food at least 3 days a week, on their own phone, with the recommended prototype (top of the page). Think aloud. Do not help. Note time from the task line to success.

TaskSuccess criterionU1U2U3U4U5
1. "How is today going?"Says calories left (901) and that protein is the furthest behind, in under 10 s
2. "Did you go over yesterday?"Opens Sun 25 and says "68 over", in 2 taps or fewer
3. "You had 1.5 bowls of yoghurt, not 1. Fix it."Saves 1.5 bowls; the total goes up by 206 kcal
4. "The flat white was at lunch. Fix it."Moves it to Lunch without deleting and re-adding
5. "You logged the water twice. Remove one."Deletes one entry and can say how to undo it
6. "Have yesterday's snack again today."Copies it to today's snack
7. "What were you eating on 9 October?"Reaches 9 Oct through the month sheet
8. "Where do your targets come from?"Opens the nutrition plan and comes back (button or edge swipe)

After the tasks: "What did you expect to happen when you held a row?" and "Was anything hard to find?" Pass mark: 4 of 5 people complete each task without help.
