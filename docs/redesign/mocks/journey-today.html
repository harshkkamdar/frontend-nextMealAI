FIQR Today

The Home journey in the chosen Spatial Depth system: every surface you meet from opening the app to logging water, with three coded options for each open decision. Every phone is live. Scroll it, tap tiles, rings and actions, swipe from the left edge to go back, drag a sheet down to close it, and switch states under each phone.

Data shapes follow the frontend types and the backend (logs summary, Log payloads, check-in status, profile targets). Built 26 Oct 2026, nothing called or written in either repo.

System rules applied, and where this page departs from the o19 mock.

  • Glass only on the control layer (tab bar, header buttons, sheets, orb, toast), cards solid white one tone step off cream (signed Q3). The o19 mock's glass cards and drifting blobs are dropped; depth comes from the push, the sheet and the header drifting slower than the feed.
  • The + orb is ink, not orange (signed Q4). That leaves one orange action per screen: the check-in card when due, Save in a sheet, Apply on the week page. The calorie ring is orange as data (Q18).
  • No macro bars (Q10): macros are ten-dot columns or rings. No sparklines on tiles except in the "Health four" baseline, which is today's app.
  • Loading is the final layout with flat placeholders at real size (no shimmer); errors sit inside the block that failed with the last good time and Retry; missing values are a small grey dash, never 0 (Q32).
  • Phones are built at a real iPhone's 390 x 844 pt and scaled to fit with a transform, so proportions match a real phone. Sizes follow the review's spec: large title 30, hero number 24, card titles 18, body 15 to 16, captions 13, labels 12, tab labels 11; gutters and card padding 16, tiles 14 at 128 tall; tab capsule and orb 52; buttons 50; rows 58; rings 112, 84 and 60. Tap-target counts are in phone points, before scaling.
  • Frame gaps: largest gap between animation frames over 900 ms after each push, scroll and sheet, Playwright in headless Chrome at 4x CPU throttle (a low-end proxy), median of three runs, after a warm-up phone. 17 ms is one frame at 60 Hz; anything over 34 ms (two dropped frames) is flagged. Single spikes vary run to run on identical phones, so a device trace is still owed (rubric item 5).
  • Time model for the numbers below: 0.9 s per tap, 0.35 s per typed character, plus reading time where the job is a glance.
