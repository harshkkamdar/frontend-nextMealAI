Ask Geo

The Geo tab, a conversation, Geo over any screen, and reporting a reply, in Spatial Depth with the Minimise to Circle tab bar. Every phone is live: type, send, tap chips, open a row, drag a sheet, swipe from the left edge to go back. The buttons above each phone switch its state.

Built from the frontend routes /chat and /chat/session, chat-thread, chat-bubble, chat-input, session-list, report-sheet, geo-companion-sheet, and the backend orchestrator (tools, action tracking, the [[choices: …]] marker, the receipt-line rule) and chat controller. Sample data follows those shapes. 26 Oct 2026.

What people come here to do

Log by talking“Had 2 eggs on sourdough and a flat white.” Geo searches, logs and echoes each item with its numbers. The most frequent job, several times a day.
Ask and decideWhat to eat next, how the week is going, swap an exercise. Geo answers, and offers 2 to 5 choices as chips when it needs a pick.
Fix what Geo didUndo, adjust, accept or change a draft plan. Rare per day, but it decides whether people trust Geo with the diary.

What reading the code turned up

  1. Only the 20 newest conversations ever show. GET /v1/chat/sessions defaults to limit 20 and the frontend sends none, so older ones are unreachable. The list below adds “Show older conversations” (next 20).
  2. Titles are the whole first message, unbounded on the backend (rename caps at 50 on the frontend only). Rows clamp to one line; the conversation header truncates.
  3. Previews can show raw markdown (last_message is stored as Geo wrote it). Strip ** and list markers before display.
  4. Dates older than a year lose the year (“3 Jan”). Show “3 Jan 2025”.
  5. A failed send leaves the message looking sent: a toast fires and the optimistic pill stays with no mark. Designed: “Didn’t send. Retry” under the pill.
  6. A failed report is silent: report-sheet catches and only clears “Sending”. Designed: an inline error and Try again.
  7. There is no token stream: POST /v1/chat returns the whole reply after every tool call. Tool-heavy turns show only dots for several seconds. Designed as one typing state and a reply that lands whole (no typewriter); a status line per tool would need backend work (out of scope, Q6).
  8. Undo is a new chat turn, not a direct delete: the chip sends “Undo” and Geo deletes. actions_taken[].details.logId is already returned, so a direct undo is possible later; every option here keeps today’s behaviour.

How the numbers were made

  • Taps are counted from the code paths (today) and from the prototype (ours). Typing is not a tap; sending is.
  • Estimated time = 1.2 s per decision + 0.5 s per tap + 0.35 s per typed character (about 35 words a minute on a phone) + 1.0 s per scroll. Geo’s own reply time is the same in every option and left out.
  • Tap targets: every control a finger can reach in every state, measured in Playwright with the element at its centre point. Phones are built at a real iPhone’s 390 × 844 pt with the owner’s sizing spec (large title 30, body 16, captions 13, 52 pt tab capsule and orb, 60 pt fields and list rows) and shown scaled to 300 px, so sizes are read back in points.
  • Orange actions: reachable controls filled with #E8622A, the highest count across the phone’s states. The calorie ring is data, not an action (Q18).
  • Max frame gap: the longest gap between animation frames while the push, scroll, sheet or send runs, in headless Chrome on a desktop machine. This is a lower bound, not the low-end Android number (F6).
  • Each action runs once to warm the layers, then is recorded; the median of 3 recorded runs is printed. The one miss: opening a conversation from the list holds one frame of about 83 ms, because the prototype builds the whole conversation in the tap’s frame. In the app, start loading the conversation on press-down (or keep the last one rendered) so the push starts on an idle frame.

Taste check

Checked against the taste skill’s app-screen rules and its tells registry. What follows the signed system is marked as such rather than changed.

  • Followed: one face (Archivo) on the sizing spec’s short scale (30, 24, 18, 16, 15, 13, 12); colour on data (calorie ring, macro dots) and on one action per screen; no icon in a tinted square anywhere; no sparkline; real zeros and real numbers that add up (432 + 105 = 537 kcal; protein 25.5 g); loading is flat bars at the final size with no shimmer; errors sit inside the block that failed, with when the data was last good and a Try again; no emoji; no eyebrow labels, index numbers or monospace; words arrive whole (no typewriter); no bounce (spring damping 0.93); the only loops are the typing dots and the upload spinner, both meaning “working” and both still under reduced motion.
  • Signed exceptions, kept: cream ground with an orange accent (brand, Q2 and Q7); glass on the controls layer and a floating capsule tab bar (C1, C2, o01); soft shadow on white cards (Q12). Content stays solid white, so glass is limited to the tab bar, round header buttons, composer, sheets and the reply menu.
  • Motion review (review-animations): fixed: the tab capsule now closes with clip-path and a transform instead of animating width and height. Kept, with reasons: page pushes and sheets run on the shared spring (about 0.5 s, no overshoot) because they move whole surfaces and retarget mid-gesture; the companion’s two heights still change height so the composer stays pinned to the sheet’s bottom (the same trade native sheets make); the corner card in C2 grows from the 44 pt button that opened it (scale 0.16 is the button’s own size, not a pop from nothing). New turns rise 8 px over 0.32 s once; history paints at rest; nothing animates on typing.
  • Relaxed on purpose: 1 px row rules inside cards (allowed on app screens), and a quiet drifting depth field behind the screens, kept at lower opacity than the o19 mock as the brief asks.
